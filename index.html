■ONLINEオンライン対戦

■Host a room and give your friend the room code, or make it public and they pick it from the list.
■Rollback netcode (GekkoNet). Input delay can be set.
■Watch a room live with its code. Matches can be recorded and played back.
■Settings: F2 (or Start + Select on a pad). Keys and pads can be rebound in game.

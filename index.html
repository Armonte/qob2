THE QUEEN OF BATTLERS 2

Kapurikon's 2D team fighting game, now in English and playable online with rollback netcode on Windows and Linux.

Download English manual Original site (JP)

The gameA team fighter where the next fighter steps in

Pick a team and fight one on one. When a fighter falls, the next one on the team steps in, and the side that runs out of fighters loses. A winner recovers some health before the next bout. Teammates waiting on the sidelines can jump in with an assist attack when you are dizzied or caught in a grab.

Arcade

Team battles against the CPU, with difficulty set in Options.

Team Play

Two players, team against team, on one machine.

Training

Set life, POW gauge, dummy action, guard, recovery and key display. F1–F7 shortcuts.

POW gauge

Fills as you attack, take hits or guard. When full, your attack rises for a while and supers unlock.

This editionWhat the new runtime adds

Online rooms

Host a room and share its code, join by code, or pick a public room from the list.

Rollback netcode

GekkoNet rollback with input delay you can set. Both players run the same simulation from inputs alone.

Spectate and replays

Watch a room live by its code, and play back recorded matches.

Windows and Linux

Native builds for both, and they play each other online.

English throughout

The menus and a full English manual, with every character's moves.

Modern controls

Keyboard and any pad, rebindable in game. F2 (or Start + Select) opens settings.

Extra Mode

Dodge
Dodges in place to avoid attacks.
Throws, however, can't be dodged.
Counter Attack
Press any attack button during a Dodge.
Counters with a unique move, invincible until it goes active.
On hit, you can cancel it into a special move!
Guard Cancel 1
While guarding
Instantly drops your guard and counters with a blowback attack.
Usable in POWER MAX mode; using it ends POWER MAX mode.
Guard Cancel 2
While guarding: cancel with a special or super
After guarding 5 or more times in a row, input a special or super command to cancel your guard.
Power Charge
Charges your power gauge yourself.

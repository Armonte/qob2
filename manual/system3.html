Advanced Mode

Emergency Roll
Rolls forward or backward.
Invincible to strikes for part of the roll, but not to throws.
You can pass through the opponent while rolling.
Tech Roll
Just before knockdown
Avoids the knockdown and gets up while moving.
Air Guard
Air attacks and projectiles can be guarded, but only during a neutral or backward jump.
Many things can't be air-guarded, such as lingering projectiles and supers.
Guard Cancel 1
While guarding
Instantly drops your guard and counters with a blowback attack.
Uses 1 stock.
Guard Cancel 2
While guarding
Instantly drops your guard and escapes forward or backward with an emergency roll.
Uses 1 stock.
Activate POWER MAX
Spend 1 stock to enter POWER MAX mode.
Supers used in this mode become powerful MAX supers.
(Needs another stock)

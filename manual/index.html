THE QUEEN OF BATTLERS 2

THE QUEEN OF BATTLERS 2 English manual

A 2D team fighting game. Pick a team, fight one on one, and when a fighter falls the next one steps in. This manual covers the screens and modes (System), every fighter's moves (Characters) and the credits (Specs).

This edition keys added by the new runtime

F2 (or Start + Select on a pad)
Settings menu: language, video, sound, controls and, in the online build, Online (rooms, host, join, watch).
F4
Controller binder (online build): choose each player's device and rebind buttons. In the standalone build F4 switches fullscreen.
F7
Game settings page.
F8 / F9
Controls page.
Alt + Enter
Fullscreen on or off.

Default keys. Standalone build: the game's own keys (1P moves with W Z A S and attacks with H J B N K M; 2P uses the arrow keys and the numpad), changed in the game's Options. Online build: W A S D move, U I punches, J K kicks, L taunt, Enter start; on a pad the D-pad moves, A B punch, X Y kick, Back taunts. Change them in the binder (F4).

The settings menu's About page opens this manual.

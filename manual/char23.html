Sakura Yae

Sakura Yae
Her special moves are all easy to use, and her combos are strong. End your combos with the Soaring Sakura Fist! Her heavy attacks are for combos, so use her light ones as anti-airs.
After the surprise Azure Sky Kick or the overhead Collarbone Breaker hits, you can link a light attack.
Her super is a multi-hit projectile you can use from any distance. It passes through everything except lingering projectiles.

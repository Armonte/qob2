Ayu Tsukimiya
Her attack power is on the low side, but she's fast. Her step (Extra) and backstep lower her stance, so she can slip under projectiles and more: she's a tricky one.
Her heavy punch has long reach and her special moves are easy to use, so use her speed and win with sheer number of hits.

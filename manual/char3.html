Misaki Kawana
Her projectile performs well, and she can fire it in the air too. All her special moves are powerful, but some leave her wide open when guarded, so choose them to suit the situation. Landing a counter throw feels especially great.
She's a little quirky and takes some intuition, but she's an all-rounder, good on both offense and defense.

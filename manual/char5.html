Nayuki Minase

Nayuki Minase
She can duck under projectiles with her low stance, dodge low attacks for a surprise strike, and even has a strike throw. Her normal moves are surprisingly easy to use, and combos built around Flowing Water Horai are strong.
And with her new moves she's become a girl who never gets out of bed... we'd like to say, but not quite. Her super is reliable even in a pinch, and chips away hard even when guarded.

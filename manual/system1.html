① Life gauge
When it reaches 0, your next waiting character takes over. The winner gets some life back. You lose when all of your waiting characters are defeated.
② Waiting characters
While a waiting character is still undefeated, a command makes them jump in with an assist attack when you are stunned or caught in a grab.
③ Time
When it reaches 0, the side with more life left wins (no life is recovered). Can be set to no time limit in the options.
④ POW gauge
Extra mode: fills when you are hit or guard, and you can charge it yourself. When full, your attack power goes up for a while and you can use a super; using one empties it.
Advanced mode: fills as you land attacks, and a little when you take damage. Up to 3 gauges can be stocked.
⑤ Hits & damage
When attacks connect in a row, shows the hit count and the damage dealt. The count carries on through a stun.

① LIFE
Life gauges full or in the red.
② POW GAUGE
Whether the power gauge is always full.
③ ACTION
What the 2P side does: STAND, SIT, JUMP, ATTACK or 2P (a second player controls it). The ATTACK CPU's strength follows the difficulty set in the options.
④ GUARD
Whether the 2P side guards. (Off while ACTION is ATTACK.)
⑤ RECOVERY
Whether the 2P side recovers from stun, at one of three speeds: speed1 normal, speed2 fast mashing, speed3 the fastest possible.
⑥ KEY CHECK
When ON, your inputs are shown at the top of the screen.
⑦ Shortcuts
The keyboard's F1–F7 change these settings directly. In this edition F2, F4 and F7 open the settings menu, the controller binder and the game settings instead, so change those three from the menu.
⑧ MaxCombo
Your highest hit count.
⑨ MaxDamage
Your highest damage.

① Difficulty
Arcade mode's difficulty. More stars: you take more damage, deal less, and the CPU gets stronger.
② Time limit
Time limit on or off. With no time limit, the winner gets no life back.
③ Controller
Joypad or keyboard. Without a joypad plugged in it shows ×, and Joypad cannot be chosen.
④ Joypad config
Rearrange the joypad buttons.
⑤ Keyboard config
Rearrange the keyboard keys.
⑥ Reset buttons
Put the button layout back to the default.
⑦ Release DirectX on exit
Whether DirectX and the like are released on exit.
⑦ To Title
Return to the title screen.

The button layout is saved automatically when you leave the options and loaded the next time the game starts.

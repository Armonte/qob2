Techniques

Master Guarding!
Attacks hit high, mid or low, and you can't block them without the matching guard. If all you do is guard, you'll miss chances to counter and get thrown, but not losing health to normal moves is the basic skill of a true battler!
Blocked with a high guard
Blocked with a low guard
Rapid-Fire Cancel
Many ground light attacks can cancel their recovery into themselves, whether they whiff or hit, so they can be repeated. Their stun values are set low to compensate, but some characters can land 5 or more hits.
Air Follow-Up
After some strike throws, or with moves that have a special follow-up property, you can sometimes hit an airborne opponent again.
After Tomoyo's strike throw, attacks connect on the launched opponent.
Overheads
Some characters have command normals that hit mid (overhead) from the ground. Of course, they can't be blocked low. They aren't all upside, though: they leave you open, for one. Air normals and some airborne specials can't be blocked low either.
An overhead like Kyou's Ice ○ Breaker
Asa-senpai's overhead
Reversal Attack
Land a reversal attack right as you get up or as guard stun ends, and "REVERSAL ATTACK" appears on screen. If you can do an invincible move the moment you wake up, it may widen your options in a fight.
